# Year 10 Science

Notes, diagrams and self-check questions for Year 10 Science: **global systems** (climate), **the universe** and **genetics revision**.

!!! info "For students"
    Each lesson page has the key ideas, worked examples and a self-check. Check-in quizzes, due dates and class materials are on QLearn.

## Global systems

| Lesson | Week | What it's about |
|---|---|---|
| [Earth's spheres, energy and a warming world](lessons/earths-spheres-and-warming.md) | 4 | Use a model of Earth's four spheres to explain how energy and carbon move around the planet, and use real data to describe how fast carbon dioxide is building up. |
| [El Niño, a warming climate and the road to net zero](lessons/el-nino-and-net-zero.md) | 4 | Explain how El Niño works and why it sits on top of long-term warming, then explain step by step how one strategy helps Australia cut its emissions. |

## The universe

| Lesson | Week | What it's about |
|---|---|---|
| [The Great Conspiracy: how do we know?](lessons/how-do-we-know.md) | 5 | Find out how people measured things they could never touch, like the size of Earth, the distance to the Sun and the distance to the stars. |
| [Practical: can light tell us what stars are made of?](lessons/practical-spectroscopy.md) | 5 | Use a spectroscope to split light from different sources, and find out how the colours in starlight reveal what a star is made of. |
| [The scale of the universe and the life of a star](lessons/scale-and-star-life-cycles.md) | 6 | Get a feel for how big the universe is, and follow a star from its birth in a cloud of gas to its death, including where the atoms in your body were made. |
| [Reading the H–R diagram](lessons/hr-diagram.md) | 6 | Learn to read the Hertzsprung–Russell diagram, the graph astronomers use to sort stars, and use it to say what kind of star something is and what it will become. |
| [Redshift and the expanding universe](lessons/redshift-and-expansion.md) | 6 | Use the Doppler effect and the spectral lines from our spectroscopy practical to explain how we know that the universe is expanding. |
| [The big bang: evidence, timeline and the fate of the universe](lessons/big-bang-evidence-and-fate.md) | 7 | Find out what the big bang theory actually says, the three main lines of evidence for it, and what the evidence suggests about how the universe will end. |
| [Technology that changed our view of the universe](lessons/technology-and-the-universe.md) | 7 | See how new technology has changed what we know about the universe, and learn how to critically analyse a development: its strengths, its limits and how much it changed our ideas. |

## Biology revision

| Lesson | Week | What it's about |
|---|---|---|
| [Revision: from DNA to Punnett squares](lessons/revision-dna-to-punnett-squares.md) | 7 | Refresh the big ideas of heredity (DNA, mutation, mitosis and meiosis) and practise Punnett squares for one trait until the method is automatic. |
| [Revision: sex-linked traits, pedigrees and evaluating claims](lessons/revision-sex-linked-and-pedigrees.md) | 7 | Revise how X-linked traits are inherited, read pedigrees to work out genotypes and inheritance patterns, and practise evaluating a claim about inheritance. |
