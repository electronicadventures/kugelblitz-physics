## Key ideas

- **Uniform circular motion**: moving in a circle at constant speed.
- The **period** T is the time for one revolution; the frequency f = 1/T.
- Speed is constant but **velocity is not**: its direction is always tangent to the circle and keeps changing.
- If the inward force is removed, the object continues in a straight line along the tangent.

!!! note "Key formulas (QCAA formula book notation)"

    \[
    v = \frac{2\pi r}{T}
    \]

    \[
    f = \frac{1}{T}
    \]


??? example "Worked example"
    **A merry-go-round of radius 2.5 m turns once every 6.0 s. Find the speed of a rider on the edge.**

    1. \(v = 2\pi (2.5)/6.0 = 2.6\) m s⁻¹

!!! warning "Common mistakes"
    - Using diameter instead of radius.
    - Saying an object at constant speed in a circle isn't accelerating.
