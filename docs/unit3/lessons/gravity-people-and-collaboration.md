## Key ideas

- **Newton** unified falling apples and orbiting moons with one law; **Émilie du Châtelet** translated and extended the *Principia*.
- **Kepler** used **Tycho Brahe's** measurements to find elliptical orbits. **Katherine Johnson** calculated trajectories for NASA's early crewed flights.
- **LIGO** (with international partners) detected gravitational waves in 2015. It took huge collaboration and extremely precise measurement.
- Projectile physics is used in **forensic** analysis (e.g. reconstructing trajectories), but courts need evidence beyond reasonable doubt, so uncertainty matters.

!!! warning "Common mistakes"
    - Treating scientific models as fixed facts: they change as evidence improves.
