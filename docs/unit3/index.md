# Unit 3: Gravity & Electromagnetism — Lesson Planner

<span class="kb-wide"></span>

!!! info "How to use this planner"
    Every Unit 3 lesson in order, from Year 11 Term 4 to Year 12 Term 2. Use it to plan ahead, revise, or catch up.
    Week numbers are a guide only: what matters is working through the checklist outcomes in order.
    **Checklist** codes (e.g. `G1.4`) match the Know-and-Do checklists on QLearn.
    **Textbook** = *Physics for Queensland Units 3 & 4* (Oxford). **CYL 2.1 Q2, 5** = *Check your learning 2.1*, questions 2 and 5.
    **Past exams** (optional extra) = QCAA external exam questions, e.g. `2023 P2 Q6` = 2023, Paper 2, Question 6 ([QCAA Physics assessment page](https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/senior/senior-subjects/sciences/physics/assessment)).

??? tip "Missed a lesson?"
    1. Open the lesson (link in *What we're learning*) and read its summary.
    2. Read the textbook section and do the *Check your learning* questions listed.
    3. Try the simulation, then check your answers. Bring anything you couldn't do to the next lesson.
    4. Missed a practical? See your teacher about catch-up data.
